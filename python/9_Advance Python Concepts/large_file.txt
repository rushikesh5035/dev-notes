Narendra Modi is the current Prime Minister of India. 

He assumed office on May 26, 2014, and is currently serving his third consecutive term after winning the 2024 general elections.

Key Details
- Incumbent: Narendra Modi.
- Assumed Office: May 26, 2014.
- Current Term: Third consecutive term (sworn in on June 9, 2024).
- Political Party: Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP).
- Official Website: PM India Official Portal.

Role and FunctionThe 

Prime Minister is the head of the government, exercising executive authority with the Union Council of Ministers. While the President of India serves as the nominal head of state, the actual executive powers rest with the Prime Minister.
